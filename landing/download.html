What you need

Before you install

Android 8.0 or newer, a Google account to sign in with, and about 40 MB free. Everything else — the five rooms, the reasoning test, the app lock — is in the build.

Read the FAQ

No Play Store account required.

Installing the APK

Tap the download, open the file, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks. That prompt appears because the app is not coming from the Play Store — it is the same permission you would grant to any direct download.

What is different on Android

Fingerprint and face unlock use the phone's own authenticator, the camera opens natively in the composer, and haptics are real taps rather than the browser's approximation.
